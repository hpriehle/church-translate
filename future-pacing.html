The packaging work is ahead of everything else. The Sunday engine and onboarding are what hold the project at V1: no real meeting has gone through the app and hub yet, and nobody but Harrison has set it up. Discovery and payment can't climb in any meaningful way until those two do.

The goal, in numbers

In numbers: 200+ congregations stream in a typical week, credit revenue covers Claude, VPS, bandwidth and Stripe with at least $800/month left over, and Harrison spends under an hour a week on it.

Reality check: the plan estimates about $4–5 profit per ward each month, so the margin only arrives at roughly 200 wards, and only if most of them buy credits instead of bringing their own free key. Mac-only also caps how many wards can join. The ladder is built to the goal as chosen.

Baseline measured 2026-11-09: 1 congregation and 1 device on the hub, 0 sessions started from the app, 8 DMG downloads in total (0.1.0: 1, 0.1.1: 2, 0.1.2: 4, 0.1.4-intel: 1).

How the project works

  1. Discovery — the Astro marketing site at hearchurch.com (home, how it works, LDS page, one blog post), sitemap and IndexNow. Hands off: a pastor or clerk who clicks "Download for Mac".
  2. Download & updates — a signed, notarized DMG from the Worker and R2 (Apple Silicon and Intel), Sparkle updates, mac/release.sh, an anonymous download counter. Hands off: an installed app that keeps itself current.
  3. Onboarding & sign-up — in the app: church, congregation, building pin, meeting times, languages; email magic link, device token, connect code for a second Mac. Hands off: a congregation registered on the hub with a confirmed location.
  4. Claude access & payment — today only the operator's own API key (or subscription token) in the Keychain; the "free first Sunday" button is a stub; no trial proxy, no billing. Hands off: permission to call Claude for this meeting.
  5. Sunday engine — on the Mac: mic → VAD → Whisper → Claude → local voices, with preflight, mic watchdog, retries and keep-awake. Hands off: captions and MP3 per language over one WebSocket.
  6. Language catalog — prompts, glossaries and voices per language; French, Wolof, Spanish and Russian released, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Persian, Nepali, Vietnamese and Chinese added 2026-11-09 but unreleased; voices download on demand. Hands off: the languages a congregation can offer, and how good each sounds.
  7. Hub relay & listeners — the VPS fans out each session; /listen finds congregations nearby, /<slug> and chapel QR codes open one directly. Hands off: people in the pews hearing the meeting in their language.
  8. Feedback & operations — download counts in D1, site analytics, per-run cost reports on the Mac, an outbox mailer, a small admin page. Hands off: what to fix and what to build next.

Build plan

Effort tags: hours, days or weeks of work. Rungs marked external depend on someone or something outside Harrison's control, so they can't be scheduled like ordinary work. Anything that touches a real meeting can only be proven on a Sunday, and deploys never happen on a Sunday.

1. Discovery: V1 → V5

  • V1 → V2 (days) — Build: the rest of the v3 URL map (/lds exists; add /small-churches, /multilingual, /languages/*, /setup, /faq, /demo), a "Get Hear Church for your church" footer on every listener page, Search Console verification and an IndexNow push. Check: the pages are indexed, and D1 shows 20+ downloads in a month that aren't Harrison's.
  • V2 → V3 (weeks, external: Google) — Build: one blog post a week aimed at a single search term, plus the /compare/* pages. Check: top-10 in Search Console for the core terms; 100+ downloads and 10+ new congregations a month. Needs listeners V2, so the listener-page footer reaches real pews.
  • V3 → V4 (weeks, external: group admins) — Build: a repeatable post-and-share kit for ward clerks and mission offices (a short video, a one-page PDF, a QR poster), with /pricing switched on. Check: 25%+ of downloads come from a non-search source; 300+ downloads a month. Needs payment V3.
  • V4 → V5 (weeks, external) — Build: whatever the referral data shows is working, doubled down on, with no outreach by hand. Check: 40+ new verified congregations a month, three months running.

2. Download & updates: V2 → V5

  • V2 → V3 (days) — Build: a release checklist step that installs the DMG under a fresh macOS user and updates from the previous version, and crash reports sent to the hub (Swift crash handler plus an engine exception hook, posted to /api/hc/report). Check: a deliberate test crash appears in the reports.
