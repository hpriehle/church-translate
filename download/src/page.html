Hear Church Find your congregation

A free Mac app that translates your church meeting live.

Hear Church listens through your Mac's microphone and translates the meeting as people speak. Listeners follow along on their own phones, with captions and a spoken voice.

Hear Church runs on a Mac. Open this page on the Mac you'll use at church to download it. Listening to a meeting? Find your congregation.

How it works

  1. Install it on the Mac at church Open the download and drag Hear Church to Applications.
  2. Set up your congregation Enter its name and where you meet, choose your languages and add your API key. Setup takes about 5 minutes. Already set up on another Mac? Connect with your congregation's code.
  3. Press Start meeting Listeners scan the QR code in the chapel or go to hearchurch.com on their phone. They have nothing to install or pay for.

What you need

Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open while a meeting is translated.

Hear Church keeps itself up to date. New versions install when you quit the app, never during a meeting.
