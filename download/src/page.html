Hear Church

Translate your meeting on your Mac.

Hear Church listens to your meeting through the Mac's microphone and translates it as people speak. Listeners follow along on their own phones, with captions and a voice in their language.

Hear Church runs on a Mac. Open this page on the Mac you'll use at church to download it. Listening to a meeting? Find your congregation.

Getting started

  1. Open the download and drag Hear Church to Applications. Then open it from Applications.
  2. Sign up with your email. Enter your congregation's name and where you meet, so listeners nearby can find you.
  3. Choose your languages. The speech model and voices download in the background while you finish setting up (about 2 GB).
  4. Press Start meeting. Listeners scan the QR code in the chapel or go to hearchurch.com on their phone.

What you need

  • Mac Apple silicon (M1 or newer)
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
  • Memory 8 GB · 16 GB for Wolof
  • Free space About 6 GB
  • Internet Wi-Fi or Ethernet during the meeting

Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open while a meeting is translated. Hear Church keeps itself up to date. New versions install when you quit the app, never during a meeting.
