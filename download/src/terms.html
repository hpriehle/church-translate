What Hear Church is

Hear Church translates a meeting by machine and streams captions and a synthetic voice to listeners' phones. Translation is paid for with prepaid credit, at the prices on hearchurch.com/pricing, and each new congregation's first hour is free. You won't pay anything without agreeing to it first.

Credits and refunds

  • What it costs. Translation costs $5 per language-hour: one hour of speech translated into one language. An hour translated into two languages is two language-hours. The first language-hour for each new congregation is free.
  • What is counted. The Mac running the meeting counts the seconds of speech in which it recognizes words. Silence and stretches with no recognized words aren't counted. Speech is only counted for a language while at least one listener is following it, by voice or captions. Nobody listening in a language means no charge for that language.
  • Buying credit. You buy credit in advance, in US dollars, from $5 to $500 at a time, by card or a card wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link) through Stripe Checkout. Credit is sold by Automaddie LLC. Stripe handles the payment, and we never see or store your card number.
  • Running out. A meeting that is already under way is never stopped for lack of credit, so your balance can go below zero. When it does, a new meeting can't start until you buy enough credit to bring the balance back above zero. What was owed comes out of that purchase.
  • Credits don't expire.
  • Refunds. You can ask for a refund of unused credit at any time by emailing support@hearchurch.com. Credit that has already been used for translation isn't refundable.

Translations can be wrong

Translation and speech recognition are automated and will make mistakes, sometimes serious ones. Don't rely on Hear Church where an error could cause harm, including for medical, legal, financial, safety or emergency information, or for formal commitments. Where accuracy matters, use a human interpreter.
