Wolof speech recognition

When someone speaks Wolof, the app understands it with models other people trained and shared. Thank you to them.

  • On Macs with Apple silicon: Kiriku-Wolof-ASR, by AI Hub Senegal (Apache 2.0), built on OpenAI's Whisper (MIT). It learned from recordings that include the Kallaama speech corpus and Google FLEURS (both CC BY 4.0).
  • On Intel Macs: Omnilingual ASR, by Meta (Apache 2.0).

Open-source software

The app includes open-source software under the GNU GPL and LGPL, among others: Piper and eSpeak NG (GPL v3), LAME and libsoxr (LGPL), and in the Intel version FFmpeg (LGPL v3) with x264 and x265 (GPL v2). Their licences and notices are in the app (Settings → About and help → Open-source notices). Their complete source code, for every version of the app, is at hearchurch.com/source; for at least three years after we last distribute a version, you may also ask support@hearchurch.com for it on a physical medium, for no more than our cost of sending it. You may replace the LGPL libraries in the app with modified versions; macOS then needs the app re-signed on your Mac: codesign --force --deep -s - "/Applications/Hear Church.app".
