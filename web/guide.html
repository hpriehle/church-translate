Live translation: running a meeting from your phone

The speaker's words are translated into French and Wolof and sent to listeners' phones. All you need is your phone (a microphone plugged into it is optional).

You need

  • A charged phone and a charger you can plug in where you sit.
  • The password (ask whoever sent you this page).
  • Optional: a microphone plugged into the phone. Otherwise place the phone close to the speaker or to a loudspeaker.

Before the meeting (5 minutes early)

  1. Open /speak and type the password. Your phone stays signed in for 30 days.
  2. Under Translate into, switch on only the languages needed today.
  3. Show listeners the link /, or put up the printed sign with its QR code (from /print).
  4. Listeners use headphones: they open the link, pick Français or Wolof, and tap the round button to hear the voice.

During the meeting

  1. Press Start speaking when the meeting begins. Allow the microphone if the phone asks.
  2. Keep this page open with the screen on. Don't lock the phone or switch to another app: the phone stops the microphone. Keep it plugged in.
  3. Press Stop when the meeting ends.
The translation runs about 5 to 10 seconds behind the speaker. That is normal.

If something goes wrong

  • "A translation run is already going": only one meeting can be translated at a time. Someone else is using it; ask them to press Stop.
  • "You are signed out": reload the page and type the password again.
  • Listeners hear nothing: check that the green bar moves when someone speaks, that their language is switched on, and ask them to tap the round button again.
  • The translation repeats itself: a listener's phone is playing out loud near your microphone. Ask them to use headphones.
